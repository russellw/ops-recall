## Summary

A new namespace shipped with `ndots:5`, so every external lookup tried five
search-domain permutations before the real name. CoreDNS QPS tripled, its cache
thrashed, and lookups intermittently timed out. checkout-api saw
`UnknownHostException` on calls to the payment gateway.

## Symptoms

- CoreDNS NXDOMAIN rate 3x baseline
- checkout-api p99 latency 2.4s, intermittent UnknownHostException
- CoreDNS pods CPU-throttled

## Root cause

`ndots:5` in the pod DNS config caused five wasted queries per external lookup.

## Remediation

1. [diagnose] Compare query volume per namespace in CoreDNS metrics — @sasha
2. [fix] Set `ndots:1` and add explicit trailing dots to external hostnames — @sasha (outcome: CoreDNS QPS back to baseline in 5 minutes)
3. [mitigate] Scale CoreDNS from 3 to 6 replicas during the fix — @sasha

## Prevention

- Namespace admission policy pinning dnsConfig ndots
