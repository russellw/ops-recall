## Summary

A change replaced a size-bounded Caffeine cache with a plain `HashMap` to "avoid
eviction latency". Heap grew until the pod was OOMKilled, restarted, warmed up,
and was killed again roughly every 11 minutes. Search latency spiked on every
restart.

## Symptoms

- search-svc pods in CrashLoopBackOff, exit code 137
- G1 Old Gen occupancy 95%+ before each kill
- p99 search latency spiking to 12s at restart

## Root cause

Unbounded in-memory cache with no eviction. Every distinct query string was
retained for the lifetime of the process.

## Remediation

1. [diagnose] Capture a heap dump from a live pod `jcmd 1 GC.heap_dump /tmp/search.hprof` — @nadia
2. [mitigate] Roll back to the previous image — @nadia (outcome: restarts stopped within 3 minutes)
3. [fix] Reinstate the bounded cache with a 200k entry ceiling and 10-minute TTL — @nadia

## Prevention

- Heap-growth alert on sustained Old Gen occupancy, independent of OOM events
