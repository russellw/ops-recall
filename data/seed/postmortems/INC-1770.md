## Summary

The wildcard certificate for `*.example.com` expired at 00:00 UTC. cert-manager
had been failing its DNS-01 challenge for 26 days because a Route53 IAM policy
change removed its write permission; the renewal failure alert was routed to a
Slack channel nobody watched. All API clients got certificate errors until a
manually issued certificate was pushed.

## Symptoms

- HTTP 526 from the CDN, NET::ERR_CERT_DATE_INVALID in browsers
- 100% of TLS handshakes failing at edge-gw-2
- cert-manager CertificateRequest in state `Failed` for 26 days

## Root cause

Expired certificate. The renewal automation had been broken since an unrelated
IAM policy tightening, and its only alerting path was an unmonitored channel.

## Remediation

1. [diagnose] Confirm the served certificate's expiry `openssl s_client -connect api.example.com:443 | openssl x509 -noout -dates` — @dan
2. [fix] Restore the Route53 write permission for the cert-manager role — @dan
3. [fix] Force renewal and reload the gateway `kubectl cert-manager renew wildcard-example-com` — @dan (outcome: valid certificate served from 00:51)

## Prevention

- Page (not Slack) at 21 days to expiry, checked from outside the cluster
