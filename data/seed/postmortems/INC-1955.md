## Summary

A downstream enrichment API slowed from 40ms to 4s p99. Consumers spent longer
than `max.poll.interval.ms` (300s) between polls, so the coordinator evicted
them; each eviction triggered a rebalance, which stopped all consumption, which
made the next poll even later. The group spent 70 minutes rebalancing rather
than consuming.

## Symptoms

- `CommitFailedException` in orders-consumer logs
- Consumer group state cycling between `PreparingRebalance` and `Stable`
- Lag rising with near-zero consumption rate
- enrichment-api p99 at 4s

## Root cause

Unbounded batch processing time combined with a slow synchronous dependency.
The consumer had no timeout on the enrichment call, so one slow batch exceeded
the poll interval.

## Remediation

1. [diagnose] Confirm rebalance storm rather than crash loop `kafka-consumer-groups --describe --group orders-consumer --state` — @rhea
2. [mitigate] Reduce `max.poll.records` from 500 to 50 — @rhea (outcome: polls returned within the interval; rebalancing stopped within 6 minutes)
3. [fix] Add a 500ms timeout and circuit breaker on the enrichment call — @rhea
4. [fix] Raise `max.poll.interval.ms` to 600s as a safety margin — @tomas

## Prevention

- Alert on consumer group state transitions, not just lag
