## Summary

A read replica was decommissioned without dropping its physical replication
slot. Postgres retained every WAL segment the slot had not consumed. Forty
hours later `/var/lib/postgresql` hit 100% and the primary refused writes with
`no space left on device`.

## Symptoms

- Writes failing on orders_db, `PANIC: could not write to file pg_wal/...`
- /var/lib/postgresql at 100% on db-prod-03
- `pg_replication_slots` showing slot `replica_2` inactive with 61 GB retained

## Root cause

Orphaned inactive replication slot pinning WAL retention.

## Remediation

1. [diagnose] List slots and their retained WAL `SELECT slot_name, active, pg_size_pretty(pg_wal_lsn_diff(pg_current_wal_lsn(), restart_lsn)) FROM pg_replication_slots` — @priya
2. [fix] Drop the orphaned slot `SELECT pg_drop_replication_slot('replica_2')` — @priya (outcome: 61 GB of WAL released within 2 minutes, writes resumed)
3. [mitigate] Expand the WAL volume by 200 GB as headroom — @dan

## Prevention

- `max_slot_wal_keep_size` set to 100 GB
- Decommission checklist includes dropping the slot
